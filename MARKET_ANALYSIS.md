## 3. CUSTOMER PAIN POINTS & USE CASES

### Primary Pain Points (by severity)

**#1: Deal Context Fragmentation** (Severity: 9/10)
- **Problem:** Calls in Google Meet, emails in Gmail, tasks in Slack, deal status in Salesforce
- **Impact:** Presales takes 15-20 min per deal just gathering context
- **Who suffers:** All presales teams (100% of market)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Unified dashboard with all context
- **Price multiplier:** 3x (customers willing to pay 3x more to solve this)

**#2: Stalled Deals Go Unnoticed** (Severity: 8/10)
- **Problem:** Deals can sit idle for weeks, managers don't know why
- **Impact:** Lost deals, missed pipeline, inaccurate forecasts
- **Who suffers:** 40% of teams (those without Gong)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Health scoring + alerts
- **Price multiplier:** 2.5x

**#3: Call Intelligence Exists But Not Integrated** (Severity: 8/10)
- **Problem:** Gong records calls but insights don't flow into deal/contact records
- **Impact:** Managers use Gong, but deals stay in Salesforce (manual work)
- **Who suffers:** 60% of mid-market (Gong customers with weak CRM)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Native Gong integration + deal context
- **Price multiplier:** 2x (eliminates manual data entry)

**#4: Forecasting is Guesswork** (Severity: 7/10)
- **Problem:** Sales reps pad numbers, managers inflate confidence; no data-driven visibility
- **Impact:** Forecast accuracy = 55-70%, missed targets, budget misallocation
- **Who suffers:** 80% of teams (endemic problem)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Forecast based on activity patterns + deal health
- **Price multiplier:** 2x (worth paying for accuracy)

**#5: Presales Work is Invisible** (Severity: 7/10)
- **Problem:** Sales managers don't see presales contributions; presales doesn't know which deals they own
- **Impact:** Presales seen as cost center, not revenue driver; misaligned incentives
- **Who suffers:** 100% of teams with presales (increasing)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Deal ownership, timeline visibility, analytics
- **Price multiplier:** 1.5x (helps with organizational alignment)

**#6: Repetitive Manual Tasks** (Severity: 6/10)
- **Problem:** Logging calls, updating deal status, creating follow-up tasks = 3-4 hours/week per SE
- **Impact:** SEs spend 30% of time on non-selling admin
- **Who suffers:** 100% of teams
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Auto-logging, automation, templates
- **Price multiplier:** 2x (time saved = revenue gained)

**#7: Customer Collaboration Inefficient** (Severity: 6/10)
- **Problem:** Sharing progress with prospects is via email/Slack; no central source of truth
- **Impact:** Prospects confused about timeline; SEs repeat info
- **Who suffers:** 80% of teams (especially enterprise deals)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Public portal + shared dashboard
- **Price multiplier:** 1.5x

**#8: Deal Velocity Unknown** (Severity: 5/10)
- **Problem:** How long do deals stay in POC? Why do some close in 2 weeks, others 6 months?
- **Impact:** Can't plan resource allocation; surprised by close rates
- **Who suffers:** 60% of teams (managers, not SEs)
- **Dealbook solution:** ✅ Analytics + trend reporting
- **Price multiplier:** 1.5x
