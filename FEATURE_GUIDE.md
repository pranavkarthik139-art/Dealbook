## 1. Dashboard & Call Prep

### What It Does
The dashboard is your command center—a personalized view of everything you need TODAY:
- **Greeting with your first name** - Makes Dealbook feel like it was built for you
- **Today's Focus** - Shows calls scheduled for today with associated deals
- **Timezone selector** - Display current time in IST and EST (or your custom zones)
- **Deal snapshot** - Quick overview of pipeline by stage
- **To-do list** - What you need to do today
- **Activity feed** - Recent updates across all your deals

### How It Saves SEs Time
**Typical SE Morning (Without Dealbook):**
- Open Gmail → Check for new emails (2-3 min)
- Open Google Calendar → Check today's calls (1-2 min)
- Open CRM → Check deal status (2 min)
- Gather context → Search emails/docs for each call (5-10 min)
- **Total: 10-18 minutes wasted every morning**

**With Dealbook:**
- Open Dealbook dashboard → Everything is right there (30 seconds)
- See all deals and calls in one place
- All context auto-populated from previous activity
- **Time Saved: 10-15 minutes EVERY morning = 50-75 min/week = 4+ hours/month**

### Real-World Example
**Before Dealbook:**
```
9:00 AM - You have a call with Acme Corp in 30 min
You spend 20 min finding:
- Last conversation notes
- Deal status (are they in POC or validation?)
- Contact names and titles
- Technical requirements from previous calls
- Competitor info they mentioned
- Pricing discussion from last call
Call starts: You're still prepping → Call quality suffers
```

**With Dealbook:**
```
9:00 AM - Dashboard shows call in 30 min
You see:
- Deal: Acme Corp (POC stage, $125K deal)
- Last call: 2 days ago (demo with CTO)
- Key contacts: Sarah (CTO), John (VP Eng)
- Next steps: Send technical requirements
- Health: 78/100 (On track)
- All on one screen, ready to go
Call starts: You're fully prepped in 2 min → Call quality improves
Result: Better conversations = more deals closed
```

### Best Practices
✅ **Check dashboard first thing every morning** (before email)
- Gets you focused on deals, not inbox stress
- Prevents meetings surprises
- Plan your day around deal activities, not emails

✅ **Use timezone selector if managing global deals**
- Pin IST and EST for US-India calls
- Always shows your perspective + customer perspective
- No more "wait, what time is it for them?" confusion

✅ **Click into calls to see full deal context**
- Contact info is there
- Previous conversations are there
- No need to search

✅ **Spend 5 min reviewing dashboard before standup**
- Know what to report to managers
- Know which deals need attention
- Have specific numbers ready

### Time Saved
- **Daily:** 10-15 min (call prep + context gathering)
- **Weekly:** 50-75 min
- **Monthly:** 4+ hours
- **Yearly:** 50+ hours

## 2. Deal Management & Kanban Pipeline

### What It Does
**Visual pipeline view** of all your deals, organized by stage:
- **Demo:** Initial discovery stage
- **POC:** Proof of concept in progress
- **Validation:** Customer validating solution
- **Closed:** Deal won/lost

**Features:**
- Drag-drop deals between stages
- See deal info on each card: name, amount, health, last activity
- Filter by status, stage, health, value
- Click any deal to see full details
- Add new deals instantly

### How It Saves SEs Time
**Typical SE Reporting (Without Dealbook):**
```
Manager: "How many deals are in POC?"
You: "Umm... let me check Salesforce"
*opens Salesforce, searches, filters*
"There are... 3 deals in POC"
Manager: "What's the total value?"
You: "Uh... let me add those up"
*manually adds numbers*
"$285,000"
Total time: 5-10 minutes for simple questions
Happens daily for managers/executives
```

**With Dealbook:**
```
Manager: "How many deals are in POC?"
You: *glances at dashboard* "3 deals, $285K total"
Instant answer → Manager happy → You're productive
Total time: 5 seconds
```

### Real-World Example
**Stage Progression That Saves Time:**

You have 8 deals:
- Demo: 3 deals ($150K)
- POC: 2 deals ($200K)
- Validation: 2 deals ($350K)
- Closed: 1 deal ($50K)

**Without Dealbook:**
- Switch between 3+ tools to verify
- Manually track where each deal is
- Can't see which ones are stalled
- Miss opportunities because visibility is poor

**With Dealbook:**
- See all 8 deals at once
- See which ones are stalled (no activity in 5+ days)
- Identify which ones need your attention NOW
- Move deals between stages with one click
- Activity auto-logs when you move them

### Best Practices
✅ **Use the Kanban view as your daily view**
- Open dashboard → See Kanban
- Spend 2 minutes reviewing
- Know exactly what to work on

✅ **Move deals when status changes**
- Just had a POC completion call? Drag to Validation
- Auto-logs the status change
- Managers always see current state
- No need for separate status update emails

✅ **Use filters when overwhelmed**
- Filter by health: "Show me red health deals" → Focus on saving deals
- Filter by value: "Show me deals over $100K" → High-value focus
- Filter by stage: "Show me demos" → New opportunity focus

✅ **Update deal amount when you learn more**
- Find out deal is bigger than expected?
- Click deal → Edit amount
- Forecast automatically updates
- Managers see real numbers

✅ **Sort deals by health (lowest first)**
- Identifies which deals need saving
- Prioritize your time on at-risk deals
- Prevent deal slippage

### Time Saved
- **Daily:** 5-10 min (pipeline review + reporting)
- **Weekly:** 30-50 min
- **Monthly:** 2-4 hours
- **Yearly:** 25-50 hours
- **Bonus:** Better deal visibility = Fewer missed opportunities = More deals closed

## 5. Deal Health Scoring

### What It Does
Automatic health score (0-100) on every deal:
- **Green (80-100):** Deal is on track, moving forward
- **Blue (60-79):** Deal is healthy but needs attention
- **Amber (40-59):** Deal is at risk, showing warning signs
- **Red (<40):** Deal is in trouble, needs immediate action

**Calculated from:**
- Activity velocity (recent calls/emails)
- Time in current stage
- Contact engagement (are they responding?)
- Last activity timestamp
- Momentum (is this getting closer or stalling?)

### How It Saves SEs Time
**Typical Deal Monitoring (Without Dealbook):**
```
You manage 15 deals
You get asked: "Which deals should we focus on?"
You: Manually review each deal
  - Check last activity: Is it recent?
  - Check stage: How long has it been here?
  - Check emails: Are they responding?
  - Check calendar: Any calls scheduled?
Time per deal: 2-3 min
Total: 30-45 min to figure out which deals are healthy

Happened weekly
= 2-3 hours/week = 8-12 hours/month
```

**With Dealbook Health Scoring:**
```
You get asked: "Which deals should we focus on?"
You: Open dashboard → Look at color coding
  - Red deals: URGENT attention needed
  - Amber deals: Follow up this week
  - Blue deals: Going well, monitor
  - Green deals: No action needed

Visual scan: 2 minutes
You know exactly which deals need focus
Without spending 45 minutes analyzing each one

Time saved: 40-45 min per week = 3-4 hours/month
```

### Real-World Example
**Your Pipeline:**
```
1. Acme Corp - $125K - Health: 85 (Green) - 2 calls last week
2. TechFlow - $65K - Health: 45 (Red) - Last contact 10 days ago
3. DataSync - $280K - Health: 72 (Blue) - In validation, waiting on their test
4. CloudWorks - $45K - Health: 20 (Red) - No response for 3 weeks
5. GlobalInc - $150K - Health: 90 (Green) - Close to closing, weekly calls
```

**Without Health Scoring:**
- You don't know which deals are dying
- TechFlow slips through the cracks for 3 more weeks before you notice
- CloudWorks gets lost in the noise
- You miss the window to save them

**With Health Scoring:**
- You see TechFlow and CloudWorks are RED immediately
- You reach out to both TODAY
- You save both deals
- Result: $110K deals saved by proactive intervention

### Best Practices
✅ **Review red health deals every morning**
- What can you do today to improve?
- Send email? Schedule call?
- Action items to prevent deal loss

✅ **Investigate sudden health drops**
- Deal was green, now it's red
- Something changed
- Reach out immediately
- Often catches issues before they become problems

✅ **Don't ignore amber deals**
- Blue/amber deals need attention
- Call/email to keep momentum
- Prevent them from dropping to red

✅ **Use health as talking point with manager**
- "I have 3 red deals I'm focusing on this week"
- Manager respects proactive approach
- Shows you're managing pipeline actively

✅ **Share health color with customer in professional way**
- Don't literally say "your deal is red health"
- But DO use health data to decide next actions
- "I want to move faster on this - let's schedule a call"
- Health score helps you decide urgency of follow-up

### Time Saved
- **Weekly:** 2-3 hours (pipeline health review)
- **Monthly:** 8-12 hours
- **Yearly:** 100-150 hours
- **Bonus:** Fewer deals slip through cracks = More deals don't stall

```
= 2-3 hours/week = 8-12 hours/month
```

```
You just finished a 45-min call with Acme Corp
Dealbook: 🤖 Auto-generates insights:
```

- Shows you were paying attention
