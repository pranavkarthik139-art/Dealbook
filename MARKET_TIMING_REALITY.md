# Hashwork: Do We Have a Market Right Now?

## The Uncomfortable Questions

### 1. Who Actually Buys This?

**Candidate 1: VP of Sales Engineering**
- Yes, they have the problem (forecasting is hard)
- No, they probably don't have a dedicated budget line for "presales tools"
- Budget likely comes from sales, which means competing with Salesforce, Gong, Navattic

**Candidate 2: VP of Sales (who oversees presales)**
- Yes, they care about win rates and cycle time
- Maybe, they might budget for this
- But they already have Salesforce + maybe Gong + maybe Vivun
- Would they add a 4th tool? Only if ROI is crystal clear

**Candidate 3: Individual Sales Engineers**
- Yes, they have the problem (want to know deal status)
- No, they can't write a check

**Reality:** No clear buyer with budget authority and incentive.

---

### 2. Is This a "Must-Have" or "Nice-to-Have"?

**Must-Have problems:**
- "I can't close deals without this" (demo automation for at-scale demos)
- "I can't forecast pipeline accurately" (conversation intelligence, basic CRM)
- "I'm wasting too much SE time" (demo automation, templates)

**Nice-to-Have problems:**
- "I'd like better visibility into deal health" (better than what they have now)
- "I'd like to know which deals will close" (predictive, not required to function)

**Hashwork is:** Nice-to-have, positioned as "better forecasting and visibility."

**Reality:** Hard to build a $100M+ company on nice-to-haves, especially when prospects are already spending $100K-500K+ on other tools.

---

### 3. Market Maturity: Is Presales Ready to Have Its Own Software Budget?

**Timeline of sales software evolution:**
- 1990s-2000s: CRM (Salesforce) emerged, owned by sales leaders
- 2010s: Sales enablement tools (Highspot, Seismic), owned by sales leaders
- 2015+: Call intelligence (Gong), owned by sales VPs
- 2018+: Presales operations (Vivun, Homerun), owned by presales leaders
- 2024+: Presales demo automation (Navattic, Naoma), owned by presales leaders

**Current reality (2026):**
- Presales is still seen as "support for sales"
- Most companies don't have a dedicated presales budget line
- SE hiring is growing, but it's ad-hoc (not strategic)
- Presales processes are still ad-hoc (not formalized)

**For Hashwork to work, presales needs to be:**
- Seen as a separate function (it's getting there)
- Have dedicated budget (only at large companies)
- Be formalized enough to measure ROI (mostly not yet)

**Reality:** Presales is 5-10 years away from being fully matured as a software category. You're early.

---

### 4. The Competitive Landscape Reality

**If you try to sell Hashwork today, what happens?**

**Large enterprise (Salesforce + Gong + Vivun):**
- "We already have deal tracking in Salesforce"
- "We already have call intelligence in Gong"
- "We already have presales metrics in Vivun"
- "Adding a 4th tool for deal intelligence feels redundant. Can't you just build it into Salesforce/Gong/Vivun?"
- **Result:** Don't buy

**Mid-market (Salesforce + Gong):**
- "We already have deal visibility in Salesforce"
- "We already have call recordings in Gong"
- "Hashwork would help with visibility, but it's not a must-have"
- "Is the ROI clear? Hard to say. Let's revisit in 6 months."
- **Result:** Don't buy (yet)

**Small company (just Salesforce):**
- "We're not sophisticated enough for this. Salesforce is enough."
- **Result:** Don't buy

**Reality:** You'll struggle to land customers because the problem you solve is "nice to have" and the tools already in place partially solve it.

---

### 5. The Category Problem

**Vivun's journey (instructive):**
- Launched 2016, mission: "Presales operations platform"
- Took 5+ years to get market traction
- Required 3 rounds of funding ($100M+) before becoming recognizable
- Only recently (2025-2026) becoming mainstream

**Why did Vivun take so long?**
- Presales was too immature as a function
- VPs of Sales Engineering didn't exist at most companies
- ROI was hard to measure ("how do we know SE effort impacts close rate?")
- Competitive alternatives (spreadsheets, ad-hoc) were "good enough"

**Hashwork risk:** You're building Vivun 2.0 on the same immature market. Different problem (deal intelligence vs. SE operations), but same market timing issue.

---

### 6. What Would Need to Be True for Hashwork to Have a Market?

**Condition 1: Presales maturity** ✗
- Companies need dedicated presales budgets
- SEs need to be seen as revenue-generating, not supporting
- POC: Only true at maybe 20% of software companies today

**Condition 2: Deal forecasting problem is acute** ?
- Most companies solve this with Salesforce + spreadsheets
- Is it painful enough to justify a new tool? Maybe at scale (500+ SEs)
- POC: Maybe true for top 5% of software companies

**Condition 3: Your solution is 10x better than alternatives** ?
- Is deal intelligence from auto-logged activity 10x better than rep confidence?
- Maybe, but it's hard to prove before buying
- POC: Unproven

**Condition 4: You can integrate with existing tool stack** ✗
- Sales leaders don't want fragmentation
- Integrating with Salesforce is hard (they control the relationship)
- Alternative: Replace something (Vivun? Gong?) — but they're entrenched
- POC: Hard to achieve

---

### 7. The Honest Assessment

**Market timing:**
- **Too early?** Maybe. Presales hasn't fully professionalized yet.
- **Too late?** Maybe. Vivun is already in market, Salesforce/Gong own the relationships.
- **Just right?** Unlikely. Usually there's a 3-5 year window, and you're probably at year 1-2 of opportunity.

**Market size today:**
- **Addressable market:** Companies with 3+ SEs = ~10,000 companies globally
- **Willing to buy specialized tools:** ~2,000 companies (top 20%)
- **Willing to buy presales deal intelligence specifically:** ~200 companies (top 2%)
- **That can clearly articulate the need right now:** ~50 companies

**Reality:** A $50 company TAM is not a VC business. It's a lifestyle business or an acquihire.

## What This Means

### Option A: Market Timing Is Fine
**If true:** There IS demand for presales deal intelligence right now
- Companies are hiring SEs aggressively
- Forecasting is a real problem
- They'll pay for solutions
- You should: Validate with 5-10 customer conversations before building more

### Option B: Market Timing Is Too Early
**If true:** Presales isn't ready for a dedicated tool yet
- Companies still see presales as supporting sales
- Budgets are limited
- ROI is hard to measure
- You should:
  - Build a minimal MVP
  - Give it away free / cheap to get traction
  - Position as "Salesforce plugin" not "standalone tool"
  - Plan for long fundraising/growth cycle (5+ years like Vivun)
  - OR find a different market problem

### Option C: Market Timing Is Too Late
**If true:** Vivun/Salesforce will own the presales space
- Vivun is already in hundreds of companies
- Salesforce will eventually build deal intelligence
- You can't compete on integration
- You should: Pivot to a different problem (maybe something adjacent)

---

## How to Know Which Is True (Right Now)

### The 10-Customer Test
Call 10 companies with 5+ SEs. Ask:

1. **"Do you use Vivun?"**
   - If yes (50%+ say yes): Market is consolidating around Vivun. You're late.
   - If no: Market might be open.

2. **"How do you forecast which deals will close?"**
   - If "Salesforce + rep gut feel": Market pain exists but no one is solving it. Opportunity.
   - If "Salesforce + Gong + Vivun analytics": Market is consolidating. Less opportunity.

3. **"Would you pay $15K/year for better deal health visibility?"**
   - If yes: Market exists
   - If "maybe": You'd need to prove ROI first
   - If no: Market doesn't exist

4. **"If Vivun added deal intelligence, would you still want a separate tool?"**
   - If yes: There's differentiation opportunity
   - If no: You're building features, not a platform

## The Honest Take

**Vivun raised $100M because:**
- Presales was hiring aggressively (10-20% YoY growth)
- Companies had no way to measure SE ROI
- There was acute pain ("we don't know how many SEs we need")

**Hashwork's pain ("which deals will close?") is:**
- Nice to have, not must have
- Partially solved by existing tools
- Hard to prove ROI on before purchase

**Unless you can find 10 companies that say "yes, we have this pain and we'd pay for it,"** you might be building for a market that doesn't exist yet.

---

## What I'd Do Next (If This Was My Company)

**Not:** Build for 12 weeks and hope for customers

**But:** Spend 2 weeks talking to 15-20 companies with 5+ SEs

Ask them:
- Who owns presales budget?
- How do they forecast?
- What's broken about current approach?
- Would they try Hashwork if it was free?
- Would they pay for it?

**If 7+ say yes to both:** You have a market. Build.

**If 3-4 say yes to both:** You have early adopters. Build for them, plan for long sales cycle.

**If <3 say yes:** You might be too early. Consider pivoting or staying small until market matures.

Or maybe you're solving a problem 3-5 years too early.

Only the 15-20 customer conversations will tell.
