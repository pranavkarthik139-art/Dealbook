# Hashwork: What You're Actually Building

## The Product (Reverse-Engineered from Your Code)

You've built a **deal intelligence platform for presales teams** that answers one core question:

**"Which deals will actually close, and which ones are at risk?"**

Not from what reps say or what stage they're in.

But from **real signals: presales activity patterns and contact engagement.**

---

## What Hashwork Actually Does

### 1. Auto-Logs Presales Activity (Zero Manual Entry)
- **Calendar:** Every call with a customer is logged automatically
- **Email:** Every customer email is captured automatically  
- **Todos:** Tasks tied to deals are tracked

**Result:** Real-time activity data flowing in automatically. No data entry friction.

### 2. Calculates Deal Intelligence in Real-Time
For each deal, Hashwork scores:
- **Health Score (0-100):** Is this deal progressing?
- **Risk Score + Risk Level:** How likely is this deal to fail? (low/medium/high/critical)
- **Momentum:** Is activity accelerating or declining? (positive/neutral/negative)
- **Contact Engagement:** Who's engaged, who's silent? (engaged/moderate/silent)
- **Key Insights:** What should you do next?

**Result:** Every deal gets a risk profile instantly, continuously updated.

### 3. Surfaces Intelligence to Presales Teams
- **Dashboard:** At-a-glance view of all deals, filtered by risk level or momentum
- **Deal Detail:** Inline alerts showing current risk, engagement issues, key insights  
- **Kanban View:** Pipeline organized by stage, but colored by health/risk
- **Filters:** Find deals by risk level, health score, momentum, value, time in stage

**Result:** Presales leaders and SEs can immediately see which deals need attention.

---

## The Core Insight (Why This Matters)

**Current reality:**
- Salesforce shows deal stage (demo → POC → negotiation → closed)
- But stage ≠ health
- You can be in "negotiation" but actually stalled (customer waiting on budget)
- You can be in "POC" but actually on track (3 of 5 milestones done, contacts responding in 24 hours)
- Deals slip through cracks because reps are optimistic and tools don't flag real risks

**Hashwork solves this:**
- **Real signal:** Contact engagement (are people responding? Are new people joining?)
- **Real signal:** Activity momentum (is activity accelerating or slowing?)
- **Real signal:** Activity consistency (are they consistently engaged or random bursts?)

**Why these signals matter:**
- A deal where 3 stakeholders are engaged, responding in <24 hours, and activity is accelerating? **Very likely to close.** Probably sooner than expected.
- A deal in "validation" stage, but no contact activity for 7 days, only 1 person engaged, and that person hasn't replied in a week? **At risk.** Customer might be stalling.

**Salesforce can't tell the difference. Hashwork can.**
